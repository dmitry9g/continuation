# Continuation

Version 0.2.0 | 18 November 2026

Read [the master directive](references/universal-directive.txt) before applying or adapting this workflow. It is the canonical portable instruction. At task start, resume after interruption/context loss, ownership handoff, or completion assessment, reconcile its version with the latest owner directions and the current checkpoint. Do not silently rely on remembered policy. A version/read receipt is traceability, not proof of compliant execution.

## Choose the requested action
- For explanation, review or tailoring, provide the requested result; do not launch sessions, send team messages or schedule work merely because the text describes doing so.
- For authorized coordination, recover actual sessions, ownership, routes, acceptance criteria and limits. Distinguish native subagents from separate Codex dialogues. Reuse the owner's roster; three responsibilities do not require exactly three sessions.
- Recover the project's designated execution coordinator. Architects advise/review, not parallel execution managers. Do not create duplicate assignments or new approval gates.

## Compose the skills, not competing plans
- **Delivery Roadmap** (`delivery-roadmap`) owns the authoritative plan, predecessor/receiver/review readiness, original estimates, recorded time and remaining forecasts. Read it when planning, reporting against or advancing an approved roadmap. Use its existing record; issue lists must be views of or links into that authoritative state, not a second editable current plan.
- **Diagnosing Bugs** (`diagnosing-bugs`) is the verified local diagnostic skill name. Read it for an actual defect or regression: symptom-specific reproduction, minimisation, falsifiable hypotheses, targeted probes, repair and regression/original-scenario verification. Do not infer that another skill named Bug Detection is identical without inspecting it.
- This skill owns cross-session coordination, shared adoption, checkpoint/resume and truthful completion levels. Delivery's completed step may have a blocked successor; a diagnostic fix is not complete shared integration.
- Verify each companion's availability and actual content in the executing environment. If absent, report that fact, use the master and applicable existing evidence for permitted independent work, and do not claim it loaded or installed. Do not install a missing skill without authorization.
- Companion suggestions for live calls, repeated stress/fuzz tests, instrumentation, cleanup or new agents remain bounded by current permissions, budgets, restrictions and ownership. Never automatically retry a denied operation. Report a reproduction/access gap honestly; continue independent allowed work rather than invent a cause or demand unavailable evidence.

## Operate and resume
Recover goal, roles, canonical revision, authorized routes, acceptance criteria, exclusions, resource accounting and explicit dated checkpoint. Keep current project data in the project record, not universal guidance.

Follow identify -> reproduce -> diagnose -> implement -> verify -> integrate -> verify consumers -> continue. Use one implementation owner per issue, small compatible integrations and isolated work state. Reuse valid evidence. Share verified reusable fixes through authorized routes and track delivery acceptance, actual receipt, adopted revision and verification in the active consumer separately.

Before yielding or dispatching a successor, reconcile readiness, ownership and remaining authority. Continue the next ready authorized action; if blocked, retain the exact dependency, owner and resumable action. Do not wake active sessions, create parallel management or duplicate work. Honor explicit pauses and hard limits.

Reading or acknowledging the policy establishes receipt, not adoption. Bind adoption to a concrete checkpoint or decision; verify behavior from a relevant decision or operation. A legitimate blocked disposition may demonstrate policy compliance at that checkpoint without proving runtime capability.

At material checkpoints retain policy version, current overrides, goal, revisions/uncommitted work, owners, evidence references, adoption/receiving states, budget and uncertain outcomes, next action and resume prerequisites. On resume, read and reconcile these with current state before acting. Never repeat an uncertain paid action.

## Continue repairs within a verified reserve
When acceptance is blocked, use Delivery Roadmap's owner-approved repair reserve, if one exists, for covered diagnosis, repair, verification, required Review, integration and handoff. Delivery owns the ledger; use the same approval, scope, units, deadline and commitments rather than creating a second budget. At start, retry, resume and handoff, reconcile the live balance and reserve the whole cycle before dispatch through the existing coordinator. Unknown/pending charges remain committed; never reuse closed/frozen balances or double-spend concurrent allocations.

Proceed without another generic acknowledgement only where current authority covers the action. Expected files are a forecast, not a hard edit boundary unless explicitly approved as one. Stop the affected work for insufficient allowance, expiry, hard exclusions or changed acceptance criteria. Explicit pauses/stops, restricted or denied actions and separately required confirmations take precedence over any reserve. If Delivery is unavailable, disclose that and recover existing authority/accounting from the verified project record; do not assume a reserve exists or claim the companion was loaded. Continue independent permitted work when a reserve-dependent action is blocked.

## Verify completion, not activity
Bind checks to the actual prepared application/revision and intended consumer. Apply the relevant positive/negative checks, required independent Review and receiving acceptance; require retained result retrieval when the deliverable calls for it. Keep each acceptance criterion and unresolved dependency visible in the existing plan.

Distinguish local/offline implementation, active consumer adoption, connected offline workflow, authorized live outcome and unattended reliability. These are separate claims, not a mandatory global ladder. Assess affected consumers only; retain justified not-applicable/deferred dispositions with an owner and revisit condition. A signature is not independent review; accepted send is not receipt; test counts or an isolated snapshot are not end-to-end success. Do not weaken validation or manufacture evidence. Scoped closure does not close a stricter parent outcome.

This skill supplies neither enforcement nor a scheduler. Reading it cannot guarantee compliance or keep a suspended session alive. Claim automatic restart/monitoring only for an actually configured and verified mechanism. Machine-checkable gates require separately authorized implementation, not a promise in this document.
